Your music, on your desktop.

Verse is a local companion. It has no analytics, advertising, account server, audio capture, or listening-history database.

Spotify

When you connect, Verse reads your current track, playback position, artwork, and device restrictions. Play, pause, and seek commands go to Spotify only when you use those controls. Spotify plays the audio.

LRCLIB and artwork

The current song’s title, primary artist, album, and duration are sent to LRCLIB to find lyrics. Those requests and artwork downloads reveal your IP address to the respective services. Lyrics stay in memory for up to one hour (128 tracks maximum). Spotify metadata and artwork are not saved to disk by Verse.

Local storage

Login tokens are encrypted using Electron safeStorage, backed by macOS Keychain, and stored in your application support directory. Tokens never enter the UI. Your public Client ID, tint, window position, and collapsed state are saved locally.

Disconnect and delete

Choose Disconnect Spotify in the Verse menu bar to delete the saved tokens, stop requests, and clear the lyrics cache and current playback data. You can also remove Verse’s access in your Spotify account’s Apps page. Remove the Verse application support folder to reset all settings.

Beta use

Verse is independent of Spotify and LRCLIB. Their respective policies apply to data sent to them. This local beta has not been approved by Spotify for distribution; synchronization and third-party lyric rights need review before release.
